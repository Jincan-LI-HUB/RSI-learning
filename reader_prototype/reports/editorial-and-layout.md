# Prototype design and editorial record

## Typesetting specification

The prototype uses a single A4 column with 29 mm side margins, 24 mm above the text and 25 mm below it. XeLaTeX sets the prose at 11.25 pt on 17.1 pt leading. The Latin face is STIX Two Text and the mathematical face is STIX Two Math. SimSun supplies Song-style Chinese prose and SimHei supplies Chinese headings and emphasis. The running header, figure labels and rules use a quiet blue-gray. The figure originals retain their semantic colors. Equations stay unnumbered where the original exposition uses no equation references. Figure and table numbers, bookmarks and a linked contents page support navigation.
